Digital Communication

Digital communication is a method of transmitting information using digital signals. Digital signals are transmitted through medias like cables, air or optical fibers to the receiver.

Some of the steps involved in the digital communication system are:

01

Information Source

It is a place from where the information originates

02

Transmitter

Converts original information into a suitable form of digital signal for transmission.

03

Modulator

Combines the digital signal with a carrier signal so that it can travel efficiently through channel.

04

Channel

Medium through which signal travels from transmitter to receiver.

05

Demodulator

Separates original information bearing signal from the carrier signal at the receiving end.

06

Receiver

Converts the received signal into its original understandable form.

07

Destination

Final place for which the data is intended.

Key Features

1

Uses discrete signals

Digital communication uses discrete signals to transmit information.

2

Binary representation

Data are transmitted in the form of binary digits (0 and 1).

3

Less affected by noise

It is less affected by noise and distortion compared to analog communication.

4

Easy data processing

It allows easy data compression, error detection and error correction.
